The first public beta of **AURA OS**, a cinematic launcher for Android TV and Google TV.

## Highlights

- Cinematic home screen: photo wallpapers with a film-style grade, large clock and greeting.
- Your apps as their own colourful logos on dark cards, with a cyan focus frame and soft glow.
- One app row you arrange in Settings, plus All apps and a Recently used row.
- Settings on the TV for wallpaper, automatic change, dimming, accent colour, card style and more.
- English and Danish. No ads, no tracking, no network access, about 4.7 MB.

## Install

Download `AURA-OS-v1.1.0-beta.1.apk` below and install it with a file manager on the TV or with
`adb install`. It opens as a normal app; making it the home screen is optional and explained in
[docs/INSTALL.md](https://github.com/Saksen2k1/aura-os/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md), including how to undo it.

## Verified on

- Android TV 8.0 emulator (API 26) and Google TV 14 emulator (API 34): install, start, all screens,
  home switch and undo.
- The design and core code have been in daily use for a week on a MediaTek Android TV 11 set as the private
  1.0 build; this beta itself has not yet run on TV hardware.
- Everything else: unverified. Device reports are very welcome.

## Known limitations

- Continue-watching with episodes is not available to third-party launchers; AURA shows recently used apps.
- Google TV and some Android TV builds need ADB to change the home app.
- Beta: expect rough edges and please report them.
