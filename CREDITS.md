# Credits

## Origin

AURA OS began as a fork of [tv-home](https://github.com/mihirgupta0900/tv-home) by Mihir Gupta (MIT).
The app-discovery, HDMI-input and focus-handling foundations come from tv-home; the design, settings,
logo handling, wallpaper pipeline and everything else were rewritten for AURA OS.

The visual direction (large clock, one app row, a content row below) was inspired by screenshots of
[Plume Launcher](https://github.com/aradhyas8/Plume_Launcher). No Plume code or assets are included.
